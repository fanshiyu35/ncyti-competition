How the competition is judged

Every edition is judged by a five-member panel appointed by the Standing Committee. Judging is independent: members serve in a personal capacity, declare any conflicts of interest, and evaluate all entries in writing against the criteria published with the call for entries.

Panel formation

  • Composition. Five members: the Chair of the Standing Committee (presiding ex officio) and four members drawn from research, higher education and practice in transport and mobility, in the Czech Republic and abroad.
  • Appointment. Members are appointed in writing by the Standing Committee before the evaluation period begins.
  • Independence. Members serve in a personal capacity and declare any conflicts of interest in relation to the entries under evaluation.

Evaluation and deliberation

  • Written evaluations. Each entry is evaluated in writing against the published criteria; evaluation records are retained by the competition office.
  • Deliberation by video conference. Panel sessions are held by video conference so that members joining from abroad can participate fully.
  • Recommendation and ratification. The panel's recommendation is submitted to the Standing Committee for ratification, after which the results are announced.
