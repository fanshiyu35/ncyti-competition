Judging Panel — 2025 (13th) Edition

The 2025 (13th) edition of the National Competition for Young Transport Innovators (NCYTI / NSMDI) was judged by a five-member panel. Members serve in a personal capacity, declare any conflicts of interest, and evaluate all entries in writing; deliberation is held by video conference so that members joining from abroad can participate fully.

Evaluation record

The Panel evaluated 22 eligible submissions in July and August 2025; its recommendation was ratified by the Standing Committee on 28 August 2025.
