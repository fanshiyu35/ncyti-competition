Thirteen editions, one idea

The competition was founded in 2013 by the Standing Committee of the National Competition for Young Transport Innovators, to give young people a serious audience for ideas about transport and mobility. It has been held every year since — the 2025 edition was the thirteenth.

How the competition has grown

EditionYearMilestone
1st2013Founding edition; the first five-member judging panel was appointed by the Standing Committee.
2nd – 4th2014 – 2016The two-category structure (Ideas; Early-Career Projects) took its present form.
5th2017Deliberation by video conference introduced, allowing panel members from abroad to participate fully.
6th – 10th2018 – 2022Entries broadened from urban mobility to regional and freight topics; partner networks began presenting winners' work.
11th – 12th2023 – 2024Judging criteria revised to reward methodological soundness alongside originality.
13th2025The 2025 panel evaluated 22 eligible submissions; its recommendation was ratified on 28 August 2025.

What the competition recognises

Winners are recognised at the annual awards meeting. Recognition covers originality, feasibility and the soundness of the analysis behind the idea; winners' work is presented to the competition's partner networks of planning and research practitioners.

The Standing Committee maintains the archive of past editions, including panel composition and evaluation records.
