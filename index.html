National Competition

Young ideas for how people and goods move — judged by the people who build the methods.

NCYTI (NSMDI) is the Czech national competition for young transport innovators. Every year, secondary-school students and early-career entrants submit ideas and projects on transport and mobility; an independent judging panel evaluates every entry in writing, and the winners are recognised at the annual awards meeting.

01

Who can enter

Two entry categories

An Ideas category for secondary-school students and an Early-Career Projects category for entrants up to age 26. Entries are submitted individually or in teams; each entry is evaluated against the published judging criteria.

02

How it is judged

A five-member independent panel

Each edition is judged by a five-member panel. Members serve in a personal capacity, declare any conflicts of interest, evaluate all entries in writing, and deliberate by video conference so that members joining from abroad can participate fully.

03

2025 (13th) edition

Judging complete

The 2025 panel evaluated 22 eligible submissions in July and August 2025; its recommendation was ratified by the Standing Committee on 28 August 2025.

About NCYTI

About the competition

NCYTI was founded in 2013 by the Standing Committee of the competition and has been held every year since — the 2025 edition was the thirteenth. The competition exists to surface practical ideas for transport and mobility from young people, and to connect the strongest entries with practitioners in planning, operations and research.

Organiser:

the Standing Committee of the National Competition for Young Transport Innovators (NCYTI Standing Committee).

Judging:

an independent five-member panel per edition; written evaluations, video-conference deliberation, ratified recommendations.

Recognition:

winners are recognised at the annual awards meeting and their work is presented to the competition's partner networks.
